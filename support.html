What's free and what's in Pro?

Flashcards, decks, spaced repetition, the daily review, importing from Quizlet or Anki, and sharing decks are free with no limits. The free plan also includes 3 quizzes a week, 3 lecture recordings, 10 scans (a PDF counts as one scan) and 3 AI tries. Pro adds unlimited lectures, scans, AI tools and quizzes, plus searchable PDF export.

How do invite codes work?

Open StudyDeck › Settings › Invite Friends and tap Get My Invite Code, then share it. When a friend enters your code, you both get 5 extra scans and 5 extra AI tries (up to 10 friends). Each person can use one friend's code. You need to be signed in to iCloud.

My lecture says "Transcribing" for a long time

Transcription happens on your iPhone and takes a few minutes for a long lecture. Keep StudyDeck open until it finishes. The first lecture in a new language also downloads Apple's speech model once, which needs an internet connection.

Summaries and "Make Flashcards" don't work

AI study tools need an iPhone that supports Apple Intelligence (iPhone 15 Pro or newer) with Apple Intelligence turned on in the iOS Settings app. On other iPhones, "Make Flashcards" still picks up lines written as "term – definition".

How do I import my Quizlet set?

In Quizlet, open the set › ⋯ › Export, keep "Tab" between term and definition, and copy the text. In StudyDeck, open a deck › ⋯ › Import Cards and paste. For Anki, use File › Export › Notes in Plain Text.

What are exam hints?

After a lecture is transcribed, StudyDeck lists the moments where your teacher said something is important or will be on the exam. Tap one to hear it, or tap Make Flashcards from These. Flashcards made from a lecture also show Hear It in Class, which plays the moment that fact was said.

How do I share a deck with a friend?

Open the deck › ⋯ › Share with a Friend, and send it by AirDrop, Messages or email. When your friend taps the file, StudyDeck opens and adds the deck. Your study progress isn't included.

Can I import lecture slides?

Yes. Tap Scan › Import a PDF and pick the file (up to 100 pages). Then use Make Flashcards or Summarize on it.

Does recording continue with the screen locked?

Yes. Recording keeps going when you lock your iPhone. A phone call pauses it; tap Resume afterwards.

How do I restore Pro on a new iPhone?

Open StudyDeck › Settings and tap Restore Purchases. Use the same Apple ID you bought it with.

How do I cancel my subscription?

Open the iOS Settings app › your name › Subscriptions › StudyDeck, and tap Cancel Subscription. Pro stays on until the end of the period you paid for. The lifetime unlock is a one-time purchase and never renews.

Where is my data stored? Is it backed up?

On your devices and, if you're signed in to iCloud, in your own private iCloud. Your decks, transcripts and scans are backed up there and appear on your iPad and Mac with the same Apple ID. You can check this in StudyDeck › Settings › Backup & Sync. Lecture audio stays only on the device that recorded it, so deleting the app there deletes its recordings. Without iCloud, everything stays on this device only.

Does StudyDeck work on iPad and Mac?

Yes. Install it from the App Store with the same Apple ID. Pro carries over, and your library syncs through iCloud. Camera scanning isn't available on Mac, but importing photos and PDFs is.
