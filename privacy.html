Privacy Policy

StudyDeck · Effective 24 November 2026

The short version

StudyDeck does not sell or share any personal data. There is no account, no advertising and no analytics or tracking. We run no servers, so your recordings, scans and flashcards never leave your iPhone. The only data stored outside your iPhone is for invite codes, and only if you use them (see below).

Microphone and lecture recordings

With your permission, StudyDeck uses the microphone only while you are recording a lecture. Recordings are saved inside StudyDeck on your iPhone. They are turned into text on your device by Apple's speech recognition. The first time you transcribe in a language, iOS may download Apple's speech model; your audio is not sent anywhere. Please follow your school's rules about recording classes.

Camera, photos and scans

StudyDeck uses the camera only when you scan notes, and reads only the photos you choose. Text is recognised on your device. Scans are stored inside StudyDeck on your iPhone.

AI study tools

Summaries and generated flashcards are made by Apple's on-device model (Apple Intelligence) on supported iPhones. Your text is processed on your iPhone and is not sent to us.

Invite codes

If you create or enter an invite code, StudyDeck stores the code and an anonymous, app-specific iCloud ID in StudyDeck's iCloud (CloudKit) database, which Apple hosts. This is used only to give you and your friend extra scans and AI tries, and to make sure each code is used once per person. It does not include your name, email or anything you study. To have this data removed, email us.

Backups

Your StudyDeck data can be included in your iPhone's own iCloud or computer backup, depending on your device settings. We have no access to these backups.

Purchases

Subscriptions and purchases are handled entirely by Apple. We do not receive your name, payment details or Apple ID. You can manage or cancel a subscription in the iOS Settings app under your name › Subscriptions.

Email

If you email us, we receive your email address and message and use them only to reply. We do not add you to any mailing list.

Children

StudyDeck does not knowingly collect any data from anyone, including children.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new effective date.
