Privacy Policy

StudyDeck · Effective 25 November 2026

The short version

StudyDeck does not sell or share any personal data. There is no account, no advertising and no analytics or tracking. We run no servers and can't see anything you study. Your library stays on your devices and, if you're signed in to iCloud, in your own private iCloud so it's backed up and shared with your iPad and Mac. Lecture audio never leaves the device that recorded it. The only data StudyDeck stores outside your iCloud is for invite codes, and only if you use them (see below).

Microphone and lecture recordings

With your permission, StudyDeck uses the microphone only while you are recording a lecture. Audio recordings are saved inside StudyDeck on the device that recorded them and are not synced or uploaded. They are turned into text on your device by Apple's speech recognition. The text transcript is part of your library (see iCloud sync). The first time you transcribe in a language, iOS may download Apple's speech model; your audio is not sent anywhere. Please follow your school's rules about recording classes.

Camera, photos and scans

StudyDeck uses the camera only when you scan notes, and reads only the photos you choose. You can also import a PDF you choose. Text is recognised on your device. Scans and imported PDFs are stored inside StudyDeck and are part of your library (see iCloud sync).

AI study tools

Summaries and generated flashcards are made by Apple's on-device model (Apple Intelligence) on supported devices. Your text is processed on your device and is not sent to us or anyone else. Exam hints and "Hear it in class" are found by matching text on your device.

iCloud sync

If you're signed in to iCloud, StudyDeck saves your subjects, decks, cards, transcripts, summaries and scans to your own private iCloud database using Apple's CloudKit. This backs up your library and keeps it the same on your iPhone, iPad and Mac. This data belongs to your iCloud account and counts towards your iCloud storage. We can't see or access it. You can turn iCloud off for StudyDeck in the iOS Settings app › your name › iCloud.

Sharing decks

When you share a deck, StudyDeck makes a file with the deck's name and cards (including card images, but not your study progress) and hands it to the app you choose, such as AirDrop or Messages. It goes only to the people you send it to; it doesn't pass through us.

Invite codes

If you create or enter an invite code, StudyDeck stores the code and an anonymous, app-specific iCloud ID in StudyDeck's iCloud (CloudKit) database, which Apple hosts. This is used only to give you and your friend extra scans and AI tries, and to make sure each code is used once per person. It does not include your name, email or anything you study. To have this data removed, email us.

Backups

Your StudyDeck data, including lecture audio, can also be included in your device's own iCloud or computer backup, depending on your device settings. We have no access to these backups.

Purchases

Subscriptions and purchases are handled entirely by Apple. We do not receive your name, payment details or Apple ID. You can manage or cancel a subscription in the iOS Settings app under your name › Subscriptions.

Email

If you email us, we receive your email address and message and use them only to reply. We do not add you to any mailing list.

Children

StudyDeck does not knowingly collect any data from anyone, including children.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new effective date.
